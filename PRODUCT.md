# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: institutions and organizations (their humas / PR staff) who buy publication packages so their stories and opinion pieces appear in credible media. Secondary: individual opinion writers (the account type `wartawan`/penulis also exists).

## Product Purpose
AJP (Aplikasi Jurnalisme Positif) lets a customer submit writing through a CMS, have it edited by the TIMES Indonesia newsroom, and get it published on TIMES Indonesia, with optional distribution to other channels depending on the package. Success = institutions subscribe to a package and publish through it.

## Positioning
Publication runs under a real newsroom: TIMES Indonesia editors guide and edit each piece, and it is published on TIMES Indonesia. Packages can bundle extra distribution channels (Instagram feed posts, e-koran editions, WA Channel posts).

## Operating Context
- Humas staff collect proof of publication (kliping) for their institution's reporting. *(Inferred from the audience; not confirmed by the user.)*
- Flow in the app: register → choose/subscribe to a package (payment via Tripay) → write in the CMS → moderation/pending review → publish.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Packages (`news_package`): price, period/jenis_periode or duration_days, quota (articles, null = unlimited), feed_instagram, ekoran, wa_channel, popular, flash_sale, level.
- Every package: publication on TIMES Indonesia, plus guidance and editing from the newsroom.
- Author badges exist.
- NOT available, never claim: comment system, article statistics dashboard.
